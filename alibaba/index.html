About Us

OOING &
MUUUNI

OOING is a Seoul-based multi-brand exporter company founded in January 2025, dedicated to bringing the highest Korean cosmetics brands to global markets.

We operate as a curated multi-brand exporter — carefully selecting brands that meet the highest standards in formulation, certification, and design.

"Beauty should feel weightless, pure, and effortless. MUUUNI is our commitment to that vision."

MUUUNI is a Seoul beauty brand built on the belief that beauty should feel weightless, pure, and effortless. Every product is formulated with CB Blending Technology to balance function and sensation.

Development History

Our Timeline

2025 Q1

OOING Co., Ltd. founded in Seoul, Korea. Established multi-brand export framework with Alibaba.com B2B platform integration.
